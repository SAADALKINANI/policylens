NPA Instruction 4000.03, Breach Response

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 4000.01 by establishing procedures for breach response. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the SAOP office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

NPA collects personally identifiable information only when it is relevant and necessary to a mission purpose. Social Security numbers may only be collected where required by law. Individuals have the right to request access to and amendment of their records. Suspected breaches of PII must be reported within one hour to the Privacy Office.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Senior Agency Official for Privacy (SAOP) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance. The Chief Information Officer (CIO) ensures information technology supports the requirements of this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

The Breach Response Team evaluates the risk of harm and determines whether notification is required. The SAOP signs each completed privacy impact assessment before it is published. The program office drafts the privacy impact assessment with support from the Privacy Office. Privacy Act requests are logged and answered within statutory timeframes.
