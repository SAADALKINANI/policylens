NPA Manual 6000.10, Telework and Workforce Flexibilities Procedures Manual

1. PURPOSE

This Manual provides step-by-step procedures for carrying out Directive 6000.01 and the related instructions Instruction 6000.02, Instruction 6000.03.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Remote work agreements change an employee's official duty station and require approval by the CHCO. Telework does not change the terms and conditions of employment or pay entitlements beyond locality. Telework eligibility is based on the duties of the position, not on the individual employee.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Human Capital Officer (CHCO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Information Officer (CIO) ensures information technology supports the requirements of this issuance. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

The employee completes telework training and submits a telework agreement to the supervisor. Supervisors review telework agreements at least annually. Requests for remote work are routed through the CHCO with a cost analysis. The Office of Human Capital reports telework participation data to the Office of Personnel Management.
