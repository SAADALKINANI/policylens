NPA Instruction 6000.03, Remote Work Agreements

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 6000.01 by establishing procedures for remote work agreements. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the CHCO office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Telework does not change the terms and conditions of employment or pay entitlements beyond locality. Telework eligibility is based on the duties of the position, not on the individual employee. Supervisors may terminate a telework agreement for documented performance or conduct reasons. Remote work agreements change an employee's official duty station and require approval by the CHCO.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Human Capital Officer (CHCO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Information Officer (CIO) ensures information technology supports the requirements of this issuance. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

Requests for remote work are routed through the CHCO with a cost analysis. The Office of Human Capital reports telework participation data to the Office of Personnel Management. The employee completes telework training and submits a telework agreement to the supervisor. Supervisors review telework agreements at least annually.
