NPA Instruction 8000.05, Enterprise Data Catalog

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 8000.01 by establishing procedures for enterprise data catalog. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the CDO office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

NPA data is a strategic asset and shall be managed throughout its lifecycle. Data sharing agreements are required before exchanging data with external organizations. Data quality standards apply to all data used for decision making and AI model training. Every dataset must have a designated data steward and be registered in the enterprise data catalog.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Data Officer (CDO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

Data quality issues are logged and tracked to resolution by the responsible steward. Requests to share data externally are reviewed by the CDO, the Privacy Office, and the OGC. Data stewards record metadata for each dataset in the catalog within 30 days of creation. The CDO publishes an annual open data plan.
