NPA Instruction 8000.04, Data Quality

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 8000.01 by establishing procedures for data quality. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the CDO office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

NPA data is a strategic asset and shall be managed throughout its lifecycle. Datasets used to train AI models must be documented, including their provenance and known limitations. Data quality standards apply to all data used for decision making and AI model training. A Data Governance Body chaired by the Chief Data Officer sets agency data standards.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Data Officer (CDO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Office of General Counsel (OGC) provides legal advice on the interpretation of this issuance. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

The CDO publishes an annual open data plan. Requests to share data externally are reviewed by the CDO, the Privacy Office, and the OGC. Data quality issues are logged and tracked to resolution by the responsible steward. Data stewards record metadata for each dataset in the catalog within 30 days of creation.
