NPA Instruction 2000.03, Records Retention Schedules

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 2000.01 by establishing procedures for records retention schedules. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the ARO office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Federal records must be managed electronically in an approved recordkeeping system. Email of senior officials is retained permanently under the Capstone approach. Every NPA employee is responsible for creating and maintaining records that document agency business. Departing employees must complete a records exit briefing before separation.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Agency Records Officer (ARO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance. The Office of General Counsel (OGC) provides legal advice on the interpretation of this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

The ARO coordinates proposed schedules with the Office of General Counsel (OGC). Supervisors confirm that departing staff have transferred records to a successor custodian. Disposition actions are documented on a records destruction certificate. Offices transfer eligible permanent records using the electronic transfer process.
