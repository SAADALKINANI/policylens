NPA Instruction 2000.02, Electronic Records

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 2000.01 by establishing procedures for electronic records. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the ARO office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Federal records must be managed electronically in an approved recordkeeping system. Every NPA employee is responsible for creating and maintaining records that document agency business. Departing employees must complete a records exit briefing before separation. Records schedules shall be reviewed at least every five years to reflect changes in business processes.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Agency Records Officer (ARO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Information Officer (CIO) ensures information technology supports the requirements of this issuance. The Office of General Counsel (OGC) provides legal advice on the interpretation of this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

Disposition actions are documented on a records destruction certificate. Supervisors confirm that departing staff have transferred records to a successor custodian. Offices transfer eligible permanent records using the electronic transfer process. Records liaisons inventory the records held by their office each fiscal year.
