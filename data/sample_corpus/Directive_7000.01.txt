NPA Directive 7000.01, Emergency Management and Continuity

1. PURPOSE

This Directive establishes policy and assigns responsibilities for the emergency management and continuity program of the National Public Administration (NPA) in accordance with 42 U.S.C. 5121, Executive Order 12656. It is implemented by Instruction 7000.02, Instruction 7000.03, Instruction 7000.04 and Manual 7000.10.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the National Public Administration (NPA), including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Every NPA facility shall have an occupant emergency plan that is exercised annually. Essential functions must be resumed within 12 hours of a continuity activation. Employees must keep their emergency contact information current in the notification system. Continuity facilities are tested at least once a year. Orders of succession and delegations of authority are maintained for all key positions. NPA maintains a continuity of operations plan to sustain essential functions during disruptions.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Director of Emergency Management (DEM) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance.
