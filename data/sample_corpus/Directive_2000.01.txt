NPA Directive 2000.01, Records Management

1. PURPOSE

This Directive establishes policy and assigns responsibilities for the records management program of the National Public Administration (NPA) in accordance with 44 U.S.C. 3101, 36 CFR 1220, OMB M-23-07. It is implemented by Instruction 2000.02, Instruction 2000.03, Instruction 2000.04, Instruction 2000.05 and Manual 2000.10.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the National Public Administration (NPA), including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Records schedules shall be reviewed at least every five years to reflect changes in business processes. Permanent records are transferred to the National Archives when they reach the end of their agency retention period. Departing employees must complete a records exit briefing before separation. Unauthorized removal or destruction of federal records must be reported to the Agency Records Officer. Email of senior officials is retained permanently under the Capstone approach. Records may only be destroyed in accordance with an approved records retention schedule.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Agency Records Officer (ARO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Office of General Counsel (OGC) provides legal advice on the interpretation of this issuance. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance.
