NPA Instruction 7000.04, Emergency Notification

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 7000.01 by establishing procedures for emergency notification. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the DEM office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Every NPA facility shall have an occupant emergency plan that is exercised annually. NPA maintains a continuity of operations plan to sustain essential functions during disruptions. Essential functions must be resumed within 12 hours of a continuity activation. Continuity facilities are tested at least once a year.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Director of Emergency Management (DEM) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

Each office reviews its essential functions and updates them after every exercise. After-action reports are completed within 45 days of an exercise or real event. The DEM activates the continuity plan on direction from the Administrator. Emergency notifications are sent by text, email, and voice within 15 minutes of a decision.
