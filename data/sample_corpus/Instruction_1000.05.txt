NPA Instruction 1000.05, Vulnerability Management

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 1000.01 by establishing procedures for vulnerability management. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the CISO office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Multifactor authentication is required for every user account with access to agency networks. Zero trust architecture principles guide the design of all new network services. Critical vulnerabilities shall be remediated within 15 calendar days of publication. Contractors operating systems on behalf of NPA must meet the same security requirements as agency staff.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Information Officer (CIO) ensures information technology supports the requirements of this issuance. The Office of General Counsel (OGC) provides legal advice on the interpretation of this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

The system owner prepares a security plan and submits it to the Authorizing Official. After an incident is contained, the response team documents lessons learned within 30 days. Account managers disable inactive accounts after 45 days without use. Vulnerability scans run weekly and results are uploaded to the agency dashboard.
