NPA Instruction 5000.03, Source Selection

1. PURPOSE

This Instruction implements Directive 5000.01 by establishing procedures for source selection. Questions about this Instruction should be directed to the SPE office.

2. APPLICABILITY

This issuance applies to all organizational elements of the NPA, including employees, contractors, and detailees performing work on behalf of the agency.

3. POLICY

Contracts for AI systems must address data rights, model documentation, and vendor lock-in. Contracting Officer Representatives (COR) must be certified before being appointed. Market research shall be conducted before issuing any solicitation. Past performance information is recorded for every contract that exceeds the reporting threshold.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES

The Senior Procurement Executive (SPE) oversees implementation of this issuance and monitors compliance across NPA. The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) identifies resources needed to carry out this issuance. The Office of Inspector General (OIG) may audit compliance with this issuance.

5. PROCEDURES

Contract files are closed out within the timeframes set by regulation. The program office prepares requirements documents and an independent government cost estimate. The technical evaluation panel rates each proposal against the stated evaluation factors. The Contracting Officer (CO) negotiates terms and awards the contract.
